Step 1
Personal relevance ranking
Existing interest score ko base banayenge. Phir freshness, engagement aur ignore history ke adjustments add karenge.


Step 2
Automatic demotion
Example: ek video 4 baar dikhayi gayi, par user ne play nahi kiya. Har ignore par score kam hoga; threshold cross hone par use temporary suppress karenge. Sirf feed fetch hona ignore nahi maana jayega.


Step 3
Har refresh par naya feed
Fresh candidates, recent impression history aur limited randomness se ranking mein controlled changes laayenge. Sirf random shuffle nahi karenge, warna relevance gir sakti hai.


Step 4
Watched aur impression filtering
Watched videos ko pehle exclude karenge; impressions se repeated exposure control hoga. Agar unseen content kam ho, toh purani impressions ka restriction pehle relax karenge.


Step 5
Smart content mixing
Personalized, related, popular aur exploration candidates ko mix karenge. Existing 80% personalized / maximum 20% popular starting point ho sakta hai; baad mein actual results se tune karenge.


Step 6
Engagement learning
Watch duration, completion, likes, comments aur skips se future ranking improve karenge. Ye pehle simple rules honge, machine-learning model nahi.


Step 7
Freshness & popularity
Popular videos ke liye 3-calendar-month cutoff rakhenge. New uploads ko bhi discovery ka chance denge.


Step 8
Performance
Useful MongoDB indexes, limited candidate pools aur aggregation filters use karenge. Pagination aur changing feed order ko bhi dhyan mein rakhenge.
